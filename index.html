Pick your chair

Six barbers, six personalities. Tap a name, get to know the man holding the clippers, then book with him.

Rome (El Jefe)

Owner · Army vet · razor man

Army vet, Muay Thai addict, resident smartass, lover of long walks off short piers, and absolutely everything his wife cooks, because he's been married long enough to know there's only one correct answer.

Rome's been cutting hair since he was 15, starting under the shade tree and eventually taking his clippers around the world, cutting hair everywhere from Korea and Thailand to Egypt and plenty of places in between.

With decades behind the chair, he's well-versed in damn near every style under the sun. Fades, tapers, shear work, beards, old-school cuts, new-school cuts. You name it. The razor is his weapon of choice, and his attention to detail is borderline unhealthy.

Book a cut, pull up a chair, and shoot the breeze. Around here, clients have a funny way of turning into family.

Book with Rome

Lino

Classic cuts · shears · fútbol

Straight outta Venezuela. Classic cuts are his bread and butter, the shears are his weapon of choice, and the kids love him.

Soccer isn't a hobby. It's a religion. Legend has it he once wrestled a soccer ball out of an alligator's mouth just to finish the game. If you love fútbol, grab his chair. Just don't talk trash about his team unless you're prepared to defend your entire bloodline.

Book with Lino

Gio

The Mullet Master · clipper work

The youngest of the crew, but don't let that fool you. The kid is nasty with the clippers.

Gio started here doing community service in high school, learned the trade, went to barber school, graduated, and came right back home. Now known as the Mullet Master, he's the go-to for younger styles and anything that needs serious clipper work.

Legend has it he used to chase dogs down the street in a Speedo to improve his speed. We never asked questions. The training clearly worked.

Book with Gio

Joe

The elder statesman · shears · stories

The elder statesman of the crew, and a comedian by trade. Joe's been cutting hair longer than some of us have been alive, and yes, he's heard the Cedric the Entertainer comparison about 47,000 times. Give him a pair of shears and he's right at home.

His signature cut? "The Republican." Clean, conservative, parted, and ready for a press conference. He's got decades behind the chair and even longer stories. Book accordingly: you might leave with a haircut and a two-drink minimum.

Book with Joe

Benji

Harleys · guitars · lights out

Benji is basically the white Joe, and Joe is the Black Benji. Don't ask. We don't make the rules.

A laid-back redneck who loves Harleys, can talk cars all damn day, and is just as likely to have a guitar in his hands as clippers. But once you hit his chair, it's lights out. The man's hands are basically NyQuil. You'll come in for a haircut, wake up 30 minutes later looking fresh, with no idea what happened.

Book with Benji

George (Dread)

Razor · clean lines · Messi

Straight outta Argentina, George is well-traveled, loves mountain biking, camping, the outdoors, and apparently Lionel Messi more than most of his immediate family.

Legend has it he once spotted Messi at Publix and abandoned a full shopping cart, his dignity, and possibly a small child just to shake his hand. Soccer runs deep, but the razor is his weapon of choice. Clean lines, sharp details, and plenty of stories from wherever the hell he's been lately. Just mention Messi and clear your schedule.

Book with George
